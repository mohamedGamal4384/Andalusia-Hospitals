# Directors High-Level Dashboard | Healthcare Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Directors High-Level Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to provide management with a comprehensive view of hospital performance across Egypt and Saudi Arabia.

The dashboard brings together financial and operational data to help monitor performance, track targets, and identify areas that require further analysis.

## 📊 Key Features

- **Financial Performance:** Actual Revenue vs. Target and Baseline.
- **Patient Volume:** Monitoring Outpatient Department (OPD) visits.
- **Medical Departments:** Analyzing performance across medical departments.
- **Digital Marketing:** Tracking revenue and visits generated through marketing campaigns.
- **Medical CRM:** Analyzing revenue and visits generated through patient follow-ups.
- **Performance Metrics:** Monitoring Cost Per Visit (CPV) and Revenue Per Patient (RPP).

## 🌍 Project Scope

- **Countries:** Egypt and Saudi Arabia.
- **Business Units:** 3 Business Units.
- **Reporting Period:** March, April, and May.
- **Analysis:** Financial and Operational Performance.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective is to transform raw healthcare data into meaningful insights, helping management understand performance, identify gaps against targets, and support data-driven decision-making.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
